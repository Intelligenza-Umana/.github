# Governance

Intelligenza Umana is currently maintained by its founder. Public repositories are readable by everyone. Anyone may discuss ideas, fork repositories and submit pull requests; maintainers review proposed changes and decide whether to approve and merge them.

Direct write access and maintainer actions are limited to organization members or people explicitly granted the required repository permissions. Membership requests may be sent to intelligenzaumana.lab@gmail.com with a GitHub username and a short description of the applicant's interests and intended contribution. The founder reviews requests in light of project needs and community principles.

The [manifesto](https://github.com/fabiozagaria/intelligenza-umana/blob/main/MANIFESTO.md) and [engineering standard](https://github.com/fabiozagaria/intelligenza-umana/blob/main/docs/ENGINEERING_STANDARD.md) guide technical decisions. AI assistance is allowed; contributors remain responsible for understanding, verification and outcomes. This lightweight model may evolve with the community.
